Before you register

  1. Identify your programme

    Identify your school, entry year, and study programme. SILS degree and exchange / one-year students may have different requirements.

  2. Check this term’s instructions

    Use the applicable official page for registration rounds, deadlines, results, and cancellation rules. Avoid relying on dates from a previous year.

  3. Check the course details

    Read the current syllabus for the instructor, language, prerequisites, timetable, and assessment. Check your own guidebook for how credits count toward your degree.

  4. Read experiences in context

    Compare the term taken, prior knowledge, and selection goal. Workload from 1 to 5 reflects a personal experience. Ask your school when eligibility or rules are unclear.
